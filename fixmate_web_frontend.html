Safety Protocol

Mandatory
  • Unplug First: Disconnect wall power and remove batteries (if safe) before every check.
  • Never Open: Sealed power supplies/adapters, microwaves, or swollen/leaking batteries.
  • Stop Immediately: If you smell burning, see smoke/sparks, or find exposed wiring, call a technician.
  • Educational beginner guidance only; not a substitute for certified repair.
